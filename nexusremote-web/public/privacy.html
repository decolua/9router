Candidate privacy

Clear data practices.
Respect for people.

This notice describes how the currently implemented NexusRemote application portal processes information. It is not a claim of legal certification. Last revised November 2026.

Data we collect

When you apply, you provide your name, email, self-reported internet speed, headset availability, workspace environment, experience level and explicit consent. The system generates an application reference and a timestamp.

Why we use it

To assess potential remote work readiness, administer candidate review and, if manually approved, create a secure portal invitation. The system does not make automatic rejection decisions. Campaign assignment requires separate verification.

Storage and sharing

Application information is stored through private backend storage on Vercel. Administrative viewing requires authenticated access. We do not sell candidate data or require applicants to pay fees. Data may be processed by the hosting provider under its service terms.

Financial and campaign information

If business integrations are later enabled, only authenticated payment records and authorized campaign events will count as financial information. The current platform does not automatically submit job applications, issue bank payouts or send WhatsApp notifications.

Retention and requests

Records are currently held until an authorized operator reviews and deletes them, subject to lawful retention requirements. An automatic retention schedule and verified business contact details are still being finalized. You may lodge an access or deletion request using the secure form below. A request will require identity verification before any record is disclosed or changed.

NexusRemote must publish its verified legal operator details, direct business contact and formal retention policy before scaling public recruitment. No job placement or earnings are promised.

Request access or deletion

Submit a request to the private administrative queue. This is not an immediate deletion or an identity verification.
